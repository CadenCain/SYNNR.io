## 2. The single hard rule

**`CAPABILITIES.md` in this repo is the source of truth for every capability
claim.** Marketing copy, DMs, posts, comments, sales lines. If a claim is not on
the DOES list, it does not get said in public. Ever.

Read it before writing anything that describes the product:
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CadenCain/SYNNR.io/main/CAPABILITIES.md`

check-in ("who took it and did it come back") and text alerts. The product does

## 4. The competitor, and how to talk about it

**IronTrac** (irontrac.com), built by **Entraca**, a McKinney TX software firm.
Caden phone-verified in July 2026 that multiple West Texas shops use it and rate
it highly. His words: "this company literally hit everything for these guys."

They do **pressure iron lifecycle**: NDT recertification, ultrasonic and
hydrostatic capture, digital pressure charts replacing Barton recorders,
MTR/CoC documents, QR tags, serialized inventory, check-in/check-out.

**They touch nothing about people.** No H2S cards, no CDLs, no medicals, no
"can this truck roll today."

**Rules for handling them:**
- Never attack IronTrac publicly. Shops like it. Talking down a tool your buyer
  already trusts makes you look small.
- Never claim SYNNR does NDT recert, pressure testing, or iron traceability.
- If a comparison comes up: they track the iron, SYNNR tracks whether the truck
  and the crew are legal to roll. Different question.

## 5. Never claim (this list is not negotiable)

**Possession tracking.** It keeps creeping back in.
- ❌ who signed a tool out, or whether it came back
- ❌ "who's got it right now"
- ❌ sign-out / return / reconciliation flows
- ❌ QR or RFID scanning, GPS, telematics

Nearest true statements: *"it says where the gear was last seen and who said
so"* and *"you can flag an asset missing and it fails the truck until it's
cleared."* Neither is a chain of custody. Last seen is a note somebody typed,
and it advertises its own age.

**Also not built:** SMS alerts (email is the live channel), automatic cert
reading from a photo without a human confirming the date, invoicing,
dispatch/scheduling, inventory counts, purchasing.

**The SMS exception.** Caden has personally promised prospects "I'll text you
myself before anything lapses." That is TRUE as a personal promise from him at
low customer count. It is FALSE as a product claim. Keep it first person
forever. Never let it become "the app texts you."

## 6. Voice

- No em-dashes. No emojis. Short sentences. Plain field talk.
- Lead with the miss, not the feature. Concrete failures pull replies;
  abstract product talk does not.
- Concede when an operator is right. The best-performing exchanges have been
  ones where Caden agreed with a critic instead of defending.
- Say "readiness check," never "pre-dispatch check." Say "gear list" (a
  reference), never "loadout checklist" (a gate).

**A capability request counts as market demand only if it is unprompted.**
Someone agreeing with a feature we described first is our own copy reflected
back at us, not evidence. This rule exists because it was broken once and a
